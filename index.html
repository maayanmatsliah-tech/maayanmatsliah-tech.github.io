Projects

The Vanishing Mutual Citation

Examined reciprocation linkage across 131.0M papers and 2.9B citations from OpenAlex (1975–2023). Contrary to expectations that digital search would increase reciprocity, mean mutual citations per paper dropped by 87.9% and the pooled rate fell 95.3%. However, interdisciplinary papers (citing 3+ fields) exceeded single-discipline papers by 3%–17% across a central band of reference deciles (10–46 references, ~58% of the corpus).

Python · DuckDB · SQL · SciPy · OpenAlex (131M Papers)
